title: ✻ Annual All-California Convention
order: 4
---

Third Sunday, and the Saturday before, in January. This two-day convention draws singers from all over the state and country and rotates every year between the San Francisco Bay Area and Los Angeles. **The next singing will take place in the Bay Area on January 16–17, 2027.**
